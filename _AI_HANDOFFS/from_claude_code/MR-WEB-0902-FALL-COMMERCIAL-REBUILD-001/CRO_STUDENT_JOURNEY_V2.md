## THE FIVE-SECOND TEST

> *"If a visitor cannot distinguish Complete from Essentials within five seconds, the build fails."*

What a visitor sees on the two cards, in reading order, before any feature list:

| | IV Prep Essentials | IV Prep Complete |
|---|---|---|
| Status | Open now | Open now · **MOST CHOSEN** |
| Pitch | *"Teach me how to interview." A focused block that gives you the framework.* | *"Stay with me through interview season." The full-season preparation system.* |
| Price | $1,199 | $2,799 |
| First two bullets | 5-Day IV Foundation Workshop · Live core sessions | Everything in Essentials · **6 Mock Interviews Included** |
| Explicit absences | *No full-season mock cadence* · *No post-interview debriefs* | — |

The distinction is carried by the pitch line and by **naming what Essentials does not include** — the fastest way to make a tier boundary legible. The absences use a distinct outline icon and a muted (but AA-compliant) colour, so they read as honest scope rather than as a downgrade.

## ROUTING BY NEED, NOT BY NAME

The need router sits immediately after the hero — before any product name appears:

| The visitor's words | Routes to |
|---|---|
| "My personal statement / application narrative" | Personal Statement Intensive — Priority |
| "Interview fundamentals" | IV Prep Essentials |
| "Full interview-season preparation" | IV Prep Complete |
| "Maximum mentorship" | 360 Match Mentorship |

No quiz. Four cards, one click. The founder ruled out gimmickry and simple guidance converts better here.

## WHY THE AIRLINE METAPHOR WAS DROPPED

The live page frames the tiers as *Business Class · First Class · Private Jet* — *"Named like airline class so the difference is obvious."*

It is memorable, but it communicates **price rank, not fit**. A visitor learns which is more expensive, which they already knew from the prices, and learns nothing about which is right for them. It also frames a $2,799 clinical-education product as a luxury upgrade.

The cards now lead with the student's own sentence instead. **This is a deliberate change and reversible in one place** — it is a founder call, not an oversight.

```
NEED ROUTER     choose without learning our names             → four routes
SEASON JOURNEY  what enrolling actually gets you              → comprehension

PROGRAMS        fit → price → what is included                → enrol

PAYMENT         Zelle best value → card → plan                → remove friction
PERSONAL STMT   Priority / Emergency, Dr. Brian               → second product
360             capacity reached, premium anchor              → 2027-28 interest
CLOSE           one recommendation                            → enrol
```

Proof appears at four separate points rather than once at the bottom: faces in the hero, the video wall, the "Live, not recorded" badge on the real class photograph, and Marian's quote beside the 360 state.
