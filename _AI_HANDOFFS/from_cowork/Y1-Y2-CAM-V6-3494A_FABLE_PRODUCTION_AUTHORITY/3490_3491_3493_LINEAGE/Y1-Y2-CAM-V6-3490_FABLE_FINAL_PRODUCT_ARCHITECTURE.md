## 1. PRODUCT DEFINITION (INVARIANT)

IV Prep On-Call is MissionMed's residency interview and communication **training engine**, living inside MissionMed Matrix. It is not an avatar app. Dr Kelly (embodiment) and Delivery Intelligence (instrumentation) are its two flagship layers riding on a pedagogy core: MissionMed interview method, SAF(e) answer structure, contextual interviewing, Film Room evidence review, Natural Gesture Fingerprint, Personal Baseline/Best, Mentor teaching tools, StoryForge seams, Vault, progression, and entitlement tiers.

**North Star:** Epic-Games-grade training engine wrapped around an Apple-grade professional interview moment.

**Core experience principle — density by state:**

| State | Density character |
|---|---|
| LOBBY / HOME / TRAINING | Game-grade: progression, modes, premium interaction |
| PREP | Focused, confidence-building, quiet |
| ACTIVE INTERVIEW | Clean, calm, professional. Never an analytics dashboard |
| DELIVERY TRAINING | Instrumentation visible — training HOW is the point |
| POST-ANSWER | Coaching/intelligence expands |
| FILM ROOM | Deep analytical flagship |
| MENTOR/ADMIN | Power instrumentation, private |

**Three densities (invariant):** SIMPLE (one huge readable instrument, no raw numbers) / STANDARD (instrument + contributors + state) / LAB (raw telemetry, confidence, provenance). LAB never contaminates SIMPLE.

**Two-Foot Test (invariant):** every live instrument answers, at 2+ feet under pressure: WHAT am I measuring / AM I okay / WHICH direction / HOW far / AM I getting closer / DID I fix it.

**Guitar-Tuner Law (invariant):** measurement → spatial relationship → target corridor → correction direction → movement toward target → satisfying lock. Live vocabulary: LOUDER ↑ · SOFTER ↓ · SLOW DOWN · MORE VARIATION · BREAK THE RHYTHM · HANDS UP · USE BOTH HANDS · RE-CENTER · RAISE CAMERA · GOOD — HOLD · LOCKED.

**Visual law (invariant):** no neon, no cyberpunk, no faint gray text, no thin traces. Dark premium environments, solid saturated AAA colors, bright white readable text, bold instrument geometry, deliberate animation, literal metric names (VOICE VOLUME, PITCH VARIATION, GESTURE HEATMAP…). No lore names for telemetry.

**Hybrid design authority:** Codex visual chassis + Opus instrument semantics + Founder intuitive-metaphor requirement. Preserved Codex donors: Gesture Field, body-relative hand territory, separate L/R trails, Camera Dock / Gravity Frame, Threadline Replay, Combo/Convergence, contributor→master composite, one-big-correction + peripheral instruments, voice target/range systems.

```

  ↓

  ↓

  1 thing that worked · 1 thing to fix · replay chip of the key moment
  ↓
SESSION END → composite + contributors (the WHY visible) → moments saved to Vault
  ↓
FILM ROOM (later) → threadline replay, opportunities, baseline overlay, personal best shadow
  ↓
NEXT MISSION generated from weakest contributor → back to ARRIVE
```

Student's question is always **"What do I fix?"** Real-time shows only corrections that are high-confidence, immediately actionable, worth the interruption, and readable in under one second. Everything deeper waits for POST/Film Room.

## 5. DELIVERY INTELLIGENCE ONTOLOGY (CENTRAL IP)

Four layers, strictly separated. Conceptual source: MissionMed's prior Vanessa Van Edwards / Science of People mapping (`RECONSTRUCTED` where the 3472 artifact is missing, constrained by the 3471C interpretation boundary).

### Layer 1 — RAW OBSERVABLE TELEMETRY
Voice: level (dBFS), volume range, volume variation, F0/pitch, median pitch, pitch range, pitch variation, pitch contour, vocal landing, pace, pace variation, cadence, cadence variation, pauses, pause placement, speech/silence, emphasis energy.
Body: head position/movement, torso posture/movement, framing center/size, orientation proxies.
Hands: L/R visibility, bilateral visibility, operating zone, territory, spatial diversity, amplitude, frequency, repetition, holds, below-frame loss, face-zone intrusion, L/R balance, speech synchrony, emphasis synchrony.

Support classes per 3471C §2 are binding: CURRENTLY SUPPORTABLE / SUPPORTABLE WITH DERIVATION / NEW ENGINEERING. Pitch/F0, cadence truth, gesture vocabulary, phase truth, and all composites are NEW ENGINEERING today. Fail-closed law: missing/stale/low-coverage input → UNAVAILABLE / SUPPRESSED / WARMING UP / visibly broken lane. Never silent interpolation. Raw media not persisted.

### Layer 2 — OBSERVABLE CUE DETECTORS
Vocal variation present/absent · monotone pattern · controlled emphasis · purposeful pause · over-regular cadence · visible-hands continuity · gesture diversity · gesture repetition · bilateral use · enumeration gesture · open-hand · comparison/contrast · illustrative gesture · emphasis beat · excessive amplitude · low territory · static hands · camera-centered presence. Gesture vocabulary (enumeration, precision, steeple-like, sweep, pointing, palms-up, palms-down, hand-over-heart, this-vs-that, convergence, self-reference…) is detected as OBSERVED GESTURE only — interpretation is a separate step.

### Layer 3 — MISSIONMED CUE PROFILES (coaching profiles, never personality declarations)
1. WARMTH-CUE PROFILE — expressive vocal variation, open-hand gestures, visible hands, responsive facial/head movement, conversational cadence, appropriate pauses, natural synchrony.
2. COMPETENCE-CUE PROFILE — controlled pace, stable projection, intentional emphasis, composed gesture range, framing stability, deliberate pauses, concise delivery.
3. CREDIBILITY/AUTHORITY-CUE PROFILE — vocal steadiness, clear vocal endings/landings, strategic pauses, purposeful gestures, low repetitive movement, cross-channel congruence. **Never inferred from truthfulness.** UI says "CREDIBILITY CUES", never "you are 87% credible."
4. CHARISMA-CUE BALANCE — not a mystery score: the visible balance of warmth-cue and competence/credibility-cue contributors. Charisma = warmth × competence made observable.
5. VOCAL VARIETY — pitch variation + volume variation + pace variation + cadence variation.
6. GESTURE EFFECTIVENESS — visibility continuity + territory + diversity + synchrony + composure.
7. CAMERA PRESENCE — dock stability + centered fraction + orientation.
8. DELIVERY CONGRUENCE — multimodal alignment of emphasis events.
9. STORYTELLING DELIVERY — phase-shaped delivery (setup–turn–payoff).

**Relationship analysis (required):** Warmth and Competence are the two primary cue axes (per VVE research). Credibility is modeled as the *stability subset* of competence cues plus congruence — it shares contributors with Competence but weights steadiness, landings, and congruence higher; the two profiles are siblings, not duplicates. Charisma is explicitly the **balance** of the two axes, not a third independent quantity — which is why WARMTH × COMPETENCE gets its own instrument family (WX) and Charisma-Cue Balance (CB) renders the same state as a balance, never as a personality total. Recommended merge: keep 4 student-facing profiles (Warmth, Competence, Charisma Balance, Congruence) and expose Credibility as a LAB/Mentor lens inside Competence until human validation justifies promotion.

### Layer 4 — HIGHER-ORDER COACHING
Phase-aware coaching (END STRONG → vocal landing, final pause, composed resolution) · Natural-vs-Interview suppression insights · Semantic Opportunity Engine (post only) · mission generation from weakest contributor · Mentor teach-this-moment.

### Composite scoring law
Default combiner is **weakest-contributor-capped weighted geometric mean** with confidence/coverage modulation:

```
master = min( Σ wᵢ·g(cᵢ) applied geometrically , cap(min cᵢ) )
cap(x): contributor in red corridor caps master at "needs work" band
confidence < τ → contributor excluded AND master shows reduced-coverage badge
```

Proof case (SCENARIO A): loud monotone — Level GOOD, Volume Variation LOW, Pitch Variation LOW → Vocal Variety master must sit in caution with `LIMITING: VOLUME VARIATION` named. Three strong contributors can never hide one seriously weak one. All weights provisional until human validation; Admin exposes and tunes them.

### Context law
No context-free universal scoring. Interpretation = QUESTION CONTEXT + ANSWER PHASE + RAW TELEMETRY + CUE. Fast pace: good in energetic lists, harmful in emotional stories and landings. Pause: powerful before an important point, problematic as repeated hesitation. Answer phases: OPEN · EXPLAIN · EXAMPLE · REFLECT · LAND (maps onto SAF(e): Start Simple→OPEN, Add Reasons→EXPLAIN, Focus on ONE Example→EXAMPLE/REFLECT, End Strong→LAND). Phase truth is NEW ENGINEERING; until then, manual/mentor markers or whole-answer view (per 3471C fail-closed law).

### SAF(e) separation law
SAF(e) is qualitative answer-STRUCTURE coaching (Start Simple · Add Reasons · Focus on ONE Example · End Strong). It is never a 1–100 grade and never conflated with Delivery Intelligence. The two meet only in the post-answer card ("structure note" + "delivery note" side by side, differently styled).

### Natural Gesture Fingerprint (differentiator)
BASELINE SESSION: mentor/student casual storytelling conversation → system records natural hand home position, territory, L/R usage, amplitude distribution, pause style, vocal variation, facial/head movement. Product renders NATURAL vs INTERVIEW overlay. Coaching output style: "Your interview delivery is significantly more physically restrained than your natural storytelling baseline" — never "you need 17% more hand gestures." Goal: *talk like yourself at your best.*

### Semantic Opportunity Engine (post/Film Room only)
Transcript semantics (enumeration, comparison, growth, inclusion, self-reference, emphasis statements) × gesture/voice telemetry → opportunity markers: POTENTIAL ENUMERATION OPPORTUNITY, COMPARISON OPPORTUNITY, EMPHASIS OPPORTUNITY, NATURAL PAUSE OPPORTUNITY, HAND-OVER-HEART EVENT. Markers are clickable replay moments, never score penalties, never live interruptions.

## 8. LIVE HUD ARCHITECTURE

Five structurally different concepts (prototype-animated):

- **HUD1 — ONE BIG CORRECTION.** Single dominant correction card (LOUDER ↑ / SLOW DOWN / GOOD — HOLD) center-bottom, plus five tiny peripheral state dots. Opus one-correction law, Codex chassis.
- **HUD2 — CORNER INSTRUMENTS.** Four fixed corner clusters (voice / pace / hands / framing), each a micro-tuner. Nothing over the stage.
- **HUD3 — BOTTOM TRAINING RAIL.** Full-width rail under the stage: corridor strip per metric, active metric expands 3×.
- **HUD4 — BODY-RELATIVE SPATIAL HUD.** Instruments anchored around the student's own camera tile: gesture field flanks the body, framing dock frames the face, voice corridor under the chin. Codex Gesture Field donor.
- **HUD5 — ADAPTIVE REVEAL.** Zero instruments at rest. The single metric currently out of corridor materializes as a tuner, resolves, lingers 2s in lock state ("GOOD — HOLD"), fades. Peripheral heartbeat dot confirms system is watching.

HUD states (all concepts): NO HUD · MINIMAL · STANDARD · COACHING. Simulation forces NO HUD. Correction engine: max one correction at a time; 8s minimum dwell between corrections; lock state must be *earned and shown* (satisfying resolution per Guitar-Tuner Law).

## 9. INNOVATION PASS (each answers What/Why/Would-they-use-it)

1. **Personal-Best Shadow** — during Delivery Training replays and (optionally) live rail, a quiet ghost marker of your personal-best value per instrument. Problem: abstract targets don't motivate. Better: competing with yourself is the strongest motivator residency applicants accept. Usage: passive, zero cost.
2. **Mentor Ghost Overlay** — Film Room overlay of mentor-marked "PERFECT" moment onto current attempt (tracks, not video), time-aligned. Problem: "do it like last Tuesday" is untransferable. Usage: mentor-driven.
3. **90-Second Micro-Missions** — generated from weakest contributor; one behavior, one corridor, one lock target ("Break the rhythm 3 times in 90 seconds"). Problem: full mock interviews are too expensive for daily practice.
4. **Before/After Synced Playback** — split Film Room playback of the same question answered pre/post coaching, tracks aligned by phase. Problem: students can't feel improvement; this makes it visible.
5. **Vocal Phrase Map** (Film Room, needs F0) — each phrase as a tile: contour arrow, level band, landing quality. The "sheet music" of the answer.
6. **Real-Time Correction Lock** — when a correction resolves, the instrument visibly locks (fill + click) and logs a `LOCK` event to the timeline; streaks of locks feed progression. Problem: live coaching usually nags; this rewards.
7. **Conversation Fingerprint Card** — one beautiful card summarizing your natural delivery signature (territory, cadence style, pitch character) from the Baseline session; the overlay reference for every NATURAL vs INTERVIEW view.
8. **Teach-This-Moment** — mentor selects exact frame span → system packages video slice + all lanes + mentor voice note into a shareable lesson chip that lands in the student's next-session prep screen.
9. **Adaptive Interviewer Challenge** (premium, later) — Dr Kelly modulates follow-up difficulty based on delivery stability, never punitively; keeps strong students in stretch zone.

Rejected as gimmicks: live emotion meters (violates interpretation boundary), public leaderboards (wrong culture for residency prep), constant gesture-vocabulary drilling (violates naturalness law).
