## Method and verification

Every entry was located online during this run (publisher or DOI page, PubMed/Europe PMC or Crossref record, open-access PDF, official model card, statute text, vendor documentation or source code). Where an entry's *existence* was verified but a specific number or detail could not be read from the fetched text, the domain reviewer marked it **UNVERIFIED (from memory)** or **[from memory]** in the claim/limitations text; those flags are preserved verbatim here. No source was fabricated. Entries are keyed by DOI/URL and merged when the same work was cited in several domains; the *Supports* column then lists each domain's claim with its tag ([R1]…[R10]). Grades follow the mission scale: **A** strong empirical support · **B** moderate evidence · **C** plausible coaching heuristic · **D** expert opinion · **E** speculative or unsupported. Blog, practitioner, vendor and statutory sources are labelled by their *Evidence type*.

**Count:** 605 unique sources. Of these, 58 entries carry an UNVERIFIED / from-memory flag on a specific figure or detail (the work itself was located), and 7 record that a page was blocked or could not be fetched — six of those are confirmed through a DOI/Crossref record or a mirror, and one (E553, an unpublished manuscript) is known only through the papers that cite it. Flags are visible in the row text so Codex and reviewers can weight them.
