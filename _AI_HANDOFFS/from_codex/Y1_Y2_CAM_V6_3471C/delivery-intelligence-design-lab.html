Teach level, pace, pause placement, emphasis, cadence variation, and eventual prosodic variety without rewarding a mechanical metronome.

Family 02

Hands as spatial control

Teach visibility, operating range, balance, repetition, and voice-linked emphasis while preserving anatomical left/right provenance.

G1 • Live field + replay heatmap

Gesture Field

Two colored trails show where the hands live; the target field turns density into a physical move.

Supportable with derivation
GOOD RANGE — HOLDEXPANDHANDS UPHANDS NOT OBSERVABLE

Teaches: hand visibility, spatial operating area, below-frame loss, face-zone avoidance, and range diversity.

Inputs: L/R present, compact hand centers/zones, shoulder scale, motion episodes. Transform: short-lived centers → decaying live trail; bounded replay bins → density. Fail: erase trail during unsafe face-count or tracking gap; never persist raw landmarks.
G2 • Bilateral range mechanic

Dual Hand Lanes

Each hand climbs its own operating lane; a bridge reveals imbalance without demanding symmetry.

Supportable with derivation
BOTH HANDS AVAILABLEUSE THE OTHER HANDHANDS IN FRAMEVISUAL SIGNAL PAUSED

Inputs: per-side hand presence, center, zone, movement episode. Transform: normalized height → lane position; availability over phase → bridge. Fail: neutral unavailable state during hand occlusion or unsafe person-specific coverage.
