## Independent design thesis

The proposed instrument language is not a collection of gauges. It treats
student behavior as controller input and uses five mechanics:

1. `TARGET ACQUISITION` — enter and hold a spatial or temporal envelope.
2. `DIRECTIONAL CORRECTION` — show the physical/vocal move, not merely error.
3. `PERIPHERAL AWARENESS` — keep instruments around the interview tile and
   reserve language for one highest-value correction.
4. `COORDINATED IMPACT` — render observable co-occurrence on one clock without
   hiding it inside a sync percentage.
5. `CONTRIBUTOR TRANSPARENCY` — reveal which observable contributor is pulling
   a configurable composite down.
