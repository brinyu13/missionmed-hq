# I1Q-3000 Canonical Design Inventory

## Meaning of “canonical” here

This is the canonicalized **archaeology index** requested by I1Q-3000: one normalized record per meaningful recovered idea. It is not a canonical product decision, build specification, medical approval, or production authorization.

Recommendation labels mean only whether an idea deserves preservation for Founder comparison:

- `PRESERVE` — retain as a candidate or requirement for later review.
- `PRESERVE WITH GATE` — retain only with the stated evidence, medical, privacy, accessibility, or architecture gate.
- `REFERENCE ONLY` — useful historical or adjacent evidence; do not copy wholesale.
- `DISCARD` — preserve the history, but do not carry the behavior forward.

## Inventory conclusion

The corpus contains multiple worthy alternatives and several explicit anti-patterns. This inventory intentionally does not collapse them into one design. Founder comparison should retain the full Ladder, the bounded Rounds thread, the truthful Daily Drills recall loop, the platform provenance model, and the adjacent review/motion patterns as separate evidence until a later authorized decision.
