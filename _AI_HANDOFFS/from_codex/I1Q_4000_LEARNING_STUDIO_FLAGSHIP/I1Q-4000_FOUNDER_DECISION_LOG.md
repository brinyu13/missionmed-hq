## 2. Prototype hypotheses

These are testable product hypotheses embodied in the build. None is treated as established fact.

- Four visibly distinct learning contracts can live in one coherent Studio without fragmenting the product.
- Exact drill-and-subject intersection is more trustworthy than silently broadening scope or repeating questions to fill a requested length.
- Quick Review is useful as a breadth and open-recall mode even when grading is self-reported.
- Board Review benefits from committing an answer and confidence before feedback appears.
- Clinical Mastery can make progression legible through a three-stage session sequence while keeping an optional Rounds branch distinct and noncanonical.
- Adaptive should explain why an item was selected and orchestrate review without becoming a separate answer renderer.
- Layered explanations can serve different review depths without forcing every learner through the longest path.
- Disconnected Replay and Zoom Notes placeholders can communicate future integration intent without implying current connectivity.
- Browser-local pause, resume, favorites, flags, and recovery can make a long Founder exploration coherent without introducing an account backend.
- Analytics are more credible when actual browser-local events are distinguished from bundled synthetic history.
- A simulated prediction can be explored safely if its seeded inputs, deterministic rule, and non-readiness boundary remain prominent.
- One responsive shell can support both focused question work and dense review dashboards without creating dead ends.

These hypotheses still require observation, content review, medical review, psychometric work, accessibility evaluation, and—in several cases—real integration evidence before any adoption claim.

## 3. Open product decisions

All three decisions below remain open. The options mirror the interactive Founder-review surface and are prompts for discussion, not recorded votes.

### Decision 01 — Learning template contracts

**Question:** Do the four templates feel meaningfully distinct without fragmenting the product?

Review options:

- Approve direction
- Revise boundaries
- Needs comparison

Evidence to consider includes the clarity of each learning contract, the amount of interaction reused across templates, and whether Clinical Mastery and Adaptive remain understandable without overstating their educational authority.

**Status:** OPEN — no Founder selection or ratification recorded in this handoff.

### Decision 02 — Prediction framing

**Question:** Is the simulated score useful while staying unmistakably separate from readiness?

Review options:

- Framing works
- Reduce prominence
- Remove from P4

Evidence to consider includes whether the disclosure is visible before interpretation, whether “current” and “rolling” can be understood as seeded demo values, and whether any score-like presentation could still be mistaken for learner readiness.

**Status:** OPEN — no Founder selection or ratification recorded in this handoff.

### Decision 03 — Replay + Zoom Notes placeholders

**Question:** Do the placeholders explain future integrations without implying they are connected?

Review options:

- Clear boundary
- Needs stronger label
- Reframe interaction

Evidence to consider includes the disconnected labels, the synthetic replay anchor and waveform, the separation of Zoom Notes from local Question Notes, and whether opening a panel could still imply a live service.

**Status:** OPEN — no Founder selection or ratification recorded in this handoff.
