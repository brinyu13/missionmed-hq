## Material hardening outcomes

- duplicate questions are never silently repeated to satisfy requested length;
- exact intersections fail honestly instead of broadening;
- confidence and answer commitment cannot be bypassed in the reducer;
- the question map cannot advance past unrevealed feedback;
- session completion requires every slot workflow to be complete;
- active/paused/completed continuity remains reachable across navigation and hydration;
- malformed checksum-valid local payloads are rejected;
- reset clears persisted and component-local review drafts;
- analytics distinguish current browser-local events from seeded fixtures;
- score displays disclose their formula/denominator and do not claim mastery/readiness;
- replay requests, verified plays, Zoom data, and local notes remain separate concepts;
- Rounds consideration is visible, durable, and idempotent.
