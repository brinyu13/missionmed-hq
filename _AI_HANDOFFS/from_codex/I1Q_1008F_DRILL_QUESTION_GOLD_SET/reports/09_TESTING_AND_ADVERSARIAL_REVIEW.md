# Testing and Adversarial Review

The final suite has 32 automated tests covering canonical serialization, Unicode code-point provenance, identity/root determinism, schema rejection, transcript/Nodes authority, jumping-in state, student-call state, rapid follow-ups, self-answer/interruption behavior, one-on-one and inferred-instructor sessions, exact eligibility-clause binding, hard meta/teaching/rhetorical exclusions, short/elliptical answer slots, resume/orphan recovery, exclusive locking, reserve-space enforcement, and a synthetic resumable 97-drill run.

Adversarial review was iterative and controlling. Nine superseded generations remain content-addressed with receipts in `TARGET_BOUNDARY/quarantine`; none was deleted or represented as final. Repairs were accepted only after the failing generation was preserved, the defect was reproduced, synthetic emitted-shard coverage was added, and the full 97-drill extraction and independent audits were rerun.

The final bounded repairs recovered 14 long-context/elliptical anchors and two additional oral-exam anchors while retaining subtractive exclusions for study checks, question/session meta, vignette coaching, demographic rhetoric, generic confirmation, and self-referential rhetoric. Restricted replay verified each of the 16 anchors exactly once in emitted question bindings.

Final results: Turing 32/32 automated tests; Sagan 194/194 provenance and 194/194 semantics; Osler 91/91 deduplicated medical-question classifications. These are engineering acceptance results, not clinical approval.
