# Research Depth Rules

## Table C - Research depth

| Research depth | Program count | Deterministic rule |
|---|---:|---|
| Deep Research | 285 | Completed provider-neutral research source; visa, resident roster, and leadership present; at least 6 of 8 configured research domains present |
| Enriched Research | 256 | Non-Deep completed research source with at least 2 configured research domains present |
| Basic Profile | 5,145 | No qualifying completed enrichment; at least 4 of 5 core registry domain groups populated |
| Research Pending | 453 | Remaining canonical identities without meaningful completed enrichment or sufficient core coverage |

The eight research domains are visa, resident roster, leadership, ABIM, fellowship inventory, IMG accessibility, DO accessibility, and Caribbean accessibility. The five core groups are official website, program structure, application timeline, visa publication, and resident composition.

Depth is workflow and coverage metadata, not a publication shortcut. It never makes review-required claim content student-visible. Parallel and Claude/Opus sources converge through the same source-type and field-coverage contract; neither provider is named or privileged in the filter predicate.

The four categories are exhaustive and mutually exclusive: `285 + 256 + 5,145 + 453 = 6,139`.
