# Research Depth After Promotion

| Depth | Programs | Deterministic meaning |
|---|---:|---|
| Deep Research | 341 | Highest current major research pass with substantial approved coverage across decision-useful domains |
| Enriched Research | 596 | At least one meaningful approved enrichment pass beyond the registry profile |
| Basic Profile | 5,201 | Core canonical registry data exists without sufficient approved enrichment for the higher tiers |
| Research Pending | 1 | Canonical identity exists but meaningful enrichment is explicitly pending/unavailable |

The four categories are mutually exclusive and sum exactly to the 6,139-program canonical registry. Depth is derived from current canonical fields and approved coverage, not from provider name or a frontend-maintained program list. Newly promoted canonical research therefore updates depth and filter intelligence without another frontend release.

No program is labeled “not researched” when upstream evidence is present: unresolved upstream evidence is represented as pending/conflict/insufficient, while approved evidence contributes to live coverage.
