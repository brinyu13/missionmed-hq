## B. Parametric rules

- Populate only confirmed applicant facts and source-bound date precision. Unknown/conflicting dates stay reviewable, never silently replaced with exact dates.

Automatic multi-year grouping is not authorized. User-authored text is not silently abbreviated or omitted.

- Last-good preview may be reused only for the same document, owner, viewer, subject and surface, with a visible stale warning. Broken saved layout must remain recoverable.
