## Two-layer architecture

1. Semantic intelligence owns event identity, dates, duration, category, institution, location, country, medical school, provenance, confidence, ambiguity, visibility, interview relevance, and chronology.
2. Presentation geometry owns x/y, width/height, rotation, crop, typography, alignment, z-order, grouping, images, flags, shapes, Color Key, profile card, year axis, background, and whitespace.

Semantic chronology proposes initial composition. It never forces presentation objects into permanent lanes or rows.

## Student workflow

1. Enter from Matrix and reach the actual Timeline app.
2. Upload or select a synthetic/authorized CV, MyERAS source, or exact File Vault version.
3. Server-side deterministic extraction plus real AI produce provenance-bound candidates.
4. High-confidence items prefill; medium-confidence items receive quick confirmation; low-confidence items ask the smallest targeted question.
5. The student bulk-accepts, edits, removes, or defers candidates.
6. Accepted events become smart freeform Timeline objects with an intelligent initial composition.
7. Builder provides continuous last-good preview; Advanced Studio provides direct manipulation.
8. All semantic and geometry state saves remotely, survives refresh/re-entry/account switching, and exports with WYSIWYG parity.
