## Why it is rejected

The Partner Demo organizes a feature inventory rather than Dr Brian's operating loop. Eleven peer destinations, KPI-first hierarchy, global student chips, repeated equal-weight rectangles, rainbow status accents, static safety text, and an embedded Student Preview compete for attention. Its fixed desktop floor fails phone/tablet/zoom use. Synthetic completeness can appear persuasive without proving identity, persistence, evidence, review, authorization, or student safety. Polishing that structure would produce a generic dark SaaS/CRM dashboard, not a MissionMed mentor command center.

## Functional ideas retained independently

Only concepts independently supported by MMC user needs and canonical contracts remain valuable:

- prepare → conduct → capture → review → follow through continuity;
- goals, milestones, tasks, commitments, open loops, sessions, memory, and history as bounded objects;
- a selected-student brief and explicit next action;
- a future separate privacy-safe student publication/agency product;
- a clearly synthetic narrative surface where useful.

These ideas would have been selected if the Partner Demo had never existed.

## Patterns that must not survive

- Feature-count navigation and KPI-first home.
- Horizontal student chips as mutable global selection.
- Separate screens duplicating one student's truth.
- Equal-weight card wallpaper and decorative AI styling.
- A universal person risk/readiness/trust score.
- Static Student View inside the mentor DOM.
- Pipeline administration inside normal mentoring.
- Fixed desktop minimum width, clipped mobile, tiny labels, and color-only state.
- Fixture or provider availability represented as real completeness.
