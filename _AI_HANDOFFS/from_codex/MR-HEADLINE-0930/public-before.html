Where Interviews Break Down

Missing the question

Recognize what the interviewer is really asking, then choose the material that answers it.

A rigid script

Use your own truthful experiences. Build answers that can move with a real conversation.

Unpracticed delivery

Work on presence, pacing, voice and body language before the stakes are real.

Generic preparation

Research programs with purpose. Connect what you learn to specific questions and better conversations.

What exactly is Interview Bootcamp Week?+

The live foundation: Orientation + Match Primer and five training days covering communication, truthful content, question strategy, interview formats, virtual and in-person readiness, program research and follow-through. It is available as a standalone focused program.

Does Complete include Interview Bootcamp Week?+

Yes. Bootcamp is the opening phase of Complete. There is never a separate Bootcamp charge, and you should not buy both. Complete continues with full-season training, mock interviews, personalized feedback and support through final interviews in February.

What if my interview is before Bootcamp ends?+

For enrolled Bootcamp Week and Complete students whose real residency interview is on or before October 18, Dr Brian will personally provide individualized emergency preparation before that interview.

Who is this for?+

IMGs, Caribbean graduates, U.S. MD and DO students, reapplicants and strong first-time applicants across specialties.

Does Bootcamp include an individual Signature Mock?+

No. Bootcamp tuition does not include an individual Signature Mock. Complete adds a mock and feedback pathway; exact arrangements are confirmed with enrollment.

How can I pay?+

Bootcamp is $549 by card or $499 by Zelle. Complete is $3,099 paid in full by card or Zelle, or $1,000 today plus six monthly $400 installments ($3,400 total). Zelle orders remain on hold, with access locked until MissionMed confirms receipt. Choose your payment method on the program detail page.

What happens after enrollment?+

Keep your order confirmation and use the same MissionMed account for My Account and your courses. Your confirmation provides schedule, placement and joining instructions. Zelle requires verified payment before activation.

What does the Complete Match Guarantee mean?+

Attend and participate in at least 80% of formally scheduled sessions for your assigned group. Absences formally excused or approved by Dr Brian do not count against attendance. If eligible and you do not Match, you enter an equivalent IV Prep Complete group in the next Match Cycle at no additional training tuition for that equivalent group. This is next-cycle training, not guaranteed placement or a refund.
