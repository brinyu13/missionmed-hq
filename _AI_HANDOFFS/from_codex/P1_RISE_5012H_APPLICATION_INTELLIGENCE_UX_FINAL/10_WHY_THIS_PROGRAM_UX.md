# Why This Program UX

Why This Program is prominent on the Overview tab and only uses source-backed differentiators. Each item carries category, specific fact, why-it-may-matter language, official source link, and retrieval date.

Live University of Michigan QA rendered specific items for the Primary Care Program, inpatient education caps, resident research symposium, care-coordinator support, PSTP pathway, QI sequence, ambulatory design, and underserved/away rotations. Programs without verified differentiators show an explicit not-yet-available state.
