# Explainable Application Match Rules

The engine emits four explicit groups:

- known blockers;
- cautions;
- positive signals;
- unknowns.

Rules are deterministic and evidence-bound. They never convert an absent published restriction into acceptance and never report match odds. Live no-profile QA correctly withheld forced fit conclusions. Automated coverage verifies Step policy, score threshold, COMLEX, attempts, YOG, USCE, visa, and composition signals plus unknown-state behavior.

Match reasons are rendered on program cards and the Fit tab when the student's profile contains enough facts; otherwise the application evidence remains visible without a personalized conclusion.
