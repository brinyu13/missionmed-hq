# Program Card Application Snapshot

Live cards display a compact, configurable decision snapshot:

- visa;
- exams/COMLEX;
- YOG;
- USCE;
- resident composition;
- research depth;
- deterministic match reason or explicit insufficient-evidence state.

The live University of Michigan IM canary rendered `Deep Research`, J-1, COMLEX Level 2, a five-year YOG window, and a published USCE policy. Card fields and priority ordering are user-configurable without changing canonical program facts.
