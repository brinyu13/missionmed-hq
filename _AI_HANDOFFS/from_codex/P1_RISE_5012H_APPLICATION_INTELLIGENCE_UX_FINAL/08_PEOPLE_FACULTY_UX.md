# People and Faculty UX

The live People tab renders structured person cards rather than raw objects. Abington QA displayed the program director, associate program directors, clerkship director, chair, geriatric-fellowship leadership, and core faculty with degrees and roles.

PD/APD/core-faculty role classification, training-history presentation, and trained-here badges are implemented and covered by tests. Missing published leadership or training history remains an explicit unavailable/research state.
