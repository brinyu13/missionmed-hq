IV Prep Complete Match Guarantee

Version: September 15, 2026. Applies to the Fall 2026 IV Prep Complete offer.

Attend and participate in at least 80% of formally scheduled sessions for your assigned group. Absences formally excused or approved by Dr. Brian do not count against attendance. If you are eligible and do not Match, you enter an equivalent IV Prep Complete group in the NEXT Match Cycle at no additional training tuition for that equivalent group.

This benefit covers the equivalent group’s training tuition for the next Match Cycle. It is not guaranteed residency placement, a refund, or unlimited years of training. It does not cover Interview Week alone or Emergency Private Interview Intensive.

This release does not alter historical purchases or the published refund policy.
