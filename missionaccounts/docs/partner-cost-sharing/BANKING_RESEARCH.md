# Bill-pay funding model for Founder review

Practical candidate: one dedicated MissionMed business checking funding pool receives partner reimbursements; the partner ledger tracks each person's contributions and coverage; authorized business bill pay pays normal vendors. Journal subledgers do not require three direct vendor payments. Whether another checking account, a bank-supported subaccount, or an existing account is suitable remains a bank/Founder decision. No account opening, bank change or payment was performed.

Chase documents business bill pay from a business checking account with scheduled payments: https://www.chase.com/digital/customer-service/helpful-tips/business-banking/payments/bill-pay-enroll-add-pay . Chase account management supports assigning user permissions by account/service: https://www.chase.com/business/support/banking/account-management . Online business banking offers Zelle: https://www.chase.com/business/banking/services/online-banking . These sources support the model; they do not establish MissionMed's eligibility, fees, recipient enrollment, account permissions or a particular subaccount feature.

Before a bank decision, Founder/banker verifies legal account owner, suitable checking product, account permissions, payment rail/limits, fees and whether separate account or subaccount is available. Keep contribution reconciliation and vendor payment reconciliation distinct. Autocollection funding is separate consent and acceptance.
