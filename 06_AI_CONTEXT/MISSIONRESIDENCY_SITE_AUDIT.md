**Q1: What Makes Mission Residency Different From Other IMG Residency Programs?**

**Q6: How Many Students Do You Accept Per Season?**

**Q7: How Do I Know If I'm Ready to Enroll?**

In this complimentary session, Dr. Brian or a team member will review your profile, assess your red flags, and give you an honest read on your chances, your strategy, and whether Mission Residency is the right fit for your situation. 1. **Step 1 — Deposit / Down Payment:** Pay a deposit to secure your enrollment. Your seat is held, and training begins immediately.

## 15. Refund & Cancellation Policy — `/refund-cancellation-policy`

**Page Title:** Refund/Cancellation Policy & Terms of Service

### Policy Summary

**Eligibility for Refund** — ALL conditions must be met simultaneously:
1. Request submitted within **30 calendar days** of initial payment
2. Request submitted **NOT within 30 days** of scheduled Orientation Day
3. You have **NOT accessed** any course materials (portal login, replays, downloads, training content)
4. You have **NOT attended** any portion of the course (advising sessions, Zoom calls, 1-on-1s)
5. Request submitted **in writing** to info@missionresidency.com from enrollment email

*(Requests via social media, text, phone, or other email addresses will not be processed)*

### Key Policy Rules
- **Deposits are non-refundable** — no exceptions
- **No refunds if payment made after course start date** — even by one day; includes late acceptances and last-minute seat releases
- **Access = commitment** — logging in, attending any session, or viewing any material makes the program fully non-refundable
- Refunds issued to original payment method within **10–15 business days**

### Policy Tone
Unusually humanized — explains the "why" behind each restriction rather than just stating rules. Builds trust while setting firm expectations:
> "We understand this may sound strict — but it's how we protect the personalized nature of our program, ensure fairness to students on the waitlist, and keep our integrity in serving serious, committed applicants."
